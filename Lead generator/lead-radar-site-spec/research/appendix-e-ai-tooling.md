# Appendix E — AI/Intelligence-Native Workflow Tooling

A positioning, copy, and visual-language audit of 6 AI-native products that Lead Radar can study without inheriting their worst tropes.

---

## 1. Glean — `glean.com`

### A. Positioning & promise
- **Hero:** "Work AI that works for all"
- **Subhead:** "Give every employee an AI Assistant and Agents that put your company's knowledge to work."
- **CTAs:** "Get a demo" / "Watch video"
- **Category:** "Work AI Platform" / "Enterprise AI"
- **Named user:** No individual persona; departments are the unit. Addresses *the buyer*, not the user.

### B. Visual language
- Color: saturated medium-bright blue, white canvas, soft gradient overlays. Reads "enterprise software."
- Type: clean geometric sans, generous tracking. No serif, no mono.
- Density: moderate; whitespace-generous.
- AI tropes: gradient text on "Work AI," soft halos, subtle sparkle-adjacent flourishes. Restrained.
- Dark mode: light is default.
- Motion: interactive department tabs.

### C. Making intelligence tangible
Leans on *enterprise breadth* over *AI magic*. Product mockups show search across systems ("100+ app connectors"), agent cards. Intelligence implied through *enterprise knowledge graph* metaphor.

### D. Information hierarchy
1. Headline → 2. Tabbed demo of department workflows → 3. Customer logos.

### E. Trust mechanics
Enterprise compliance language. *"Glean saves up to 110 hours per user/year."* Permissions implied. No founder visibility.

### F. Magic moment
Tabbed demos rather than single moment.

### G. Voice
- "Work AI that works for all"
- "Find what you need, wherever it lives"
- "Glean saves up to 110 hours per user/year"

**Earned:** 110-hour stat. **Cringe-leaning:** "Work AI that works for all" — committee tongue-twister.

### H. Pricing
Fully gated. "Get a demo" only.

### I. Founder craft
Low. No founder photos, no manifesto. Reads as enterprise vendor.

## 2. Cursor — `cursor.com` (Anysphere)

### A. Positioning & promise
- **Hero:** "Built to make you extraordinarily productive, Cursor is the best way to code with AI."
- **CTAs:** "Download for macOS," "Try mobile agent," "Request a demo"
- **Category:** "the best way to code with AI" — refuses the "IDE" frame
- **Named user:** *Celebrity testimonials* — Jensen Huang, Andrej Karpathy, Patrick Collison, Greg Brockman, shadcn. Named user is "a brilliant person."

### B. Visual language
- Color: near-black canvas with chromatic gradient backdrops (purple/teal/orange). Code-editor aesthetic in marketing.
- Type: modern geometric sans, tight tracking; mono inside product surfaces only.
- Density: spacious, oversize hero, generous whitespace.
- AI tropes: gradient *backdrops* (not gradient text). Subtle glows. No sparkle icons.
- Dark mode: default. Canonical dark-mode AI-native brand.
- Motion: spring-based, restrained; interactive demo previews.

### C. Making intelligence tangible
**Show the IDE actually working.** Hero is a live Cursor desktop demo with real project states ("In Progress," "Ready for Review"). Intelligence shown as *output*, not iconography. The only one of the six where marketing surface IS product surface.

### D. Information hierarchy
1. Headline → 2. Interactive Cursor demo → 3. Celebrity-engineer wall.

### E. Trust mechanics
Celebrity engineers, not citations or compliance badges. Reliability implied via "Complete codebase understanding," semantic search, human-review hand-offs ("handing off tasks to Cursor, while you focus on making decisions"). Cursor presents as *research team*, not personality cult.

### F. Magic moment
Yes — one above-fold interactive product demo.

### G. Voice
- "Built to make you extraordinarily productive"
- "Agents turn ideas into code"
- "Works autonomously, runs in parallel"
- "Magically accurate autocomplete"
- "Predict your next action"

**Earned:** "Predict your next action" — describes actual behavior. **Cringe-leaning:** "Magically accurate" — used once sparingly.

### H. Pricing
Public, transparent. Hobby (Free) → Individual ($20) → Teams ($40/user/mo) → Enterprise. AI cost openly framed: *"Every plan includes a set amount of model usage. On-demand usage allows you to continue using models after your included amount is consumed, billed in arrears."*

### I. Founder craft
**Highest in the set.** Dark-mode-default; oversized hero type; IDE-on-homepage; restrained gradients; celebrity-engineer wall; per-request economics. The decision to fork VS Code is the loudest craft signal.

## 3. Hex — `hex.tech`

### A. Positioning & promise
- **Hero:** "The AI Analytics Platform for your whole team"
- **Subhead:** "Finally — anyone in your business can get trusted data insights, from advanced analytics to simple questions, in one integrated platform."
- **CTAs:** "Get started for free" + "Request a demo"
- **Category:** "AI analytics platform" / "agentic analytics"

### B. Visual language
- Color: cool, near-monochrome. Whites, grays, near-black.
- Type: modern sans with confident bold weights for stats.
- Density: light at top, densifies on scroll.
- **AI tropes: most overt of the six.** Wand emoji 🪄 in announcement banner. *"Bringing the magic of AI to data."* Gradient/glowing text. **Warning case for Lead Radar.**
- Dark mode: not default; light dominant.
- Motion: animated state changes — "Thought for 23 seconds" delays, agent "thinking" cues.

### C. Making intelligence tangible
**Real conversational query with answer beneath.** Example: "Can you show me NexaCorp's Q3 sales by product line?" → auto-generated analysis, chart, insight. Product *answers a question on the page*.

### D. Information hierarchy
1. Rotating announcement banner with wand emoji → 2. AI conversation mockup → 3. "Agentic workflows" feature blocks.

### E. Trust mechanics
**One of the best trust patterns:** *"Threads can leverage this context in your workspace to generate more accurate, reliable answers. The data team can create semantic models, endorse trusted data, and write AI guides."* And: *"Every conversation in Threads becomes a Hex project that the data team can open as a notebook for help, inspection, or deeper analysis. Dig into the results with full transparency into the agent's reasoning."*

AI output is back-traceable to a notebook a human can inspect.

### F. Magic moment
Chat-with-data mockup.

### G. Voice
- "Finally — anyone in your business can get trusted data insights"
- "Bringing the magic of AI to data"
- "Agentic workflows to empower your entire team"
- "Build trust in data with semantic models and AI governance"

**Earned:** "Build trust in data with semantic models." **Cringe:** wand emoji + "magic of AI." Earns it back with transparency messaging.

### H. Pricing
Public, tiered. Community (free) → Professional ($36/Editor/mo) → Team ($75/Editor/mo) → Enterprise. AI cost = compute credits scaled per tier.

### I. Founder craft
Mid. Polished mockups, clean type — but wand emoji dilutes signal. Transparency-into-reasoning is the strongest move.

## 4. Notion AI — `notion.com/product/ai`

### A. Positioning & promise
- **Hero:** "Meet your 24/7 AI team"
- **Subhead:** "Infinite minds, built for teamwork. Answer questions, prioritize tasks, and write reports—all while you sleep."
- **CTAs:** "Try for free" / "Request a demo"
- **Category:** "AI workspace" with agents that "automate busywork"

### B. Visual language
- Color: predominantly dark with blue/purple accents. Notable shift from Notion's classic warm-cream — AI page goes night-mode.
- Type: clean sans with gradient text effects on key phrases.
- AI tropes: animated GIFs showing AI "faces," gradient overlays, glowing UI accents. Multiple hero GIFs running simultaneously.
- Dark mode: default. Departure from main Notion brand.
- Motion: four hero GIFs demonstrating distinct agent actions.

### C. Making intelligence tangible
*Agents-as-coworkers* — "24/7 AI team" framing treats AI like a roster. Most anthropomorphizing of the six. Risk: ages badly.

### D. Information hierarchy
1. "Meet your 24/7 AI team" hero → 2. Enterprise logo wall (OpenAI as a customer logo) → 3. Four-up animated demo grid.

### E. Trust mechanics
Logo wall dominant. **OpenAI as customer** is the single most powerful social proof in the set. Customer quote from Ramp adds operator credibility.

### F. Magic moment
Four parallel animated demos.

### G. Voice
- "Meet your 24/7 AI team"
- "Infinite minds, built for teamwork"
- "Answer questions, prioritize tasks, and write reports — all while you sleep"
- "If it's something that's repetitive, we have a Notion Agent for it"
- "All our teams, docs, and workflows are in Notion, and it's listening to Slack"

**Cringe-leaning:** "Infinite minds" + "24/7 team" anthropomorphize. **Earned:** "It's listening to Slack."

### H. Pricing
Public, tiered. Free / Plus €9.50 / Business €19.50 / Enterprise. **Credit pricing for agents publicly named:** *"$10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits."*

### I. Founder craft
Low-to-mid. AI page reads as *campaign*, not founder-craft.

## 5. Perplexity — `perplexity.ai`

(Homepage 403'd to WebFetch — synthesized from Smith & Diction case study, Off Menu brand analysis.)

### A. Positioning & promise
- **Hero tagline:** "Where knowledge begins"
- **Complementary:** "Ask questions and trust the answers"
- **Category:** "Answer engine" — explicitly *not* chatbot, *not* search engine
- **Named user:** None — anyone with a question. Brand is *universal*.

### B. Visual language
- Color: deliberately *invisible*. Team instruction: "the colors needed to almost be completely invisible." Light/dark modes with minimal accent. Explicitly moved *away* from "traditional reflex blue."
- Type: **FK Display** for the wordmark (subtle edge cuts and soft intersections), **FK Grotesk** for body. Lowercase "p" — deliberate choice to mirror how users actually type a search query.
- Density: extremely sparse. Home product surface is a single input box.
- **AI tropes: none.** No sparkles. No gradient text. No glow. *The anti-AI-cringe benchmark.*
- Dark mode: available; both modes first-class.

### C. Making intelligence tangible
**The citation IS the proof of intelligence.** Sources pulled from bottom-of-answer to top-of-answer — deliberate UX shift to *make trust visible*. You don't see "AI thinking" — you see numbered citations, instantly inspectable.

### D. Information hierarchy
1. Search input → 2. Suggested queries → 3. Recent discovery feed.

Product IS the home page. No marketing-vs-product split.

### E. Trust mechanics
**Strongest in the set.** Three signals: (1) inline citations on every answer, (2) "Ask questions and trust the answers" as brand pillar, (3) stated decision to go subscription-first to *"preserve user trust in the answer engine, prioritizing objective results over ad revenue."* Brand chose lower revenue to protect trust-loop.

### F. Magic moment
The *answer itself*. No marketing demo because marketing surface IS product.

### G. Voice
- "Where knowledge begins"
- "Ask questions and trust the answers"
- "Know it all" (merch)

**Earned, almost entirely.** Brand explicitly references "vintage Apple ads," "World's Fair," "Swiss design principles with warmer tones and a hint of green" as influences — none AI-aesthetic references. Phi Hoang's instruction: avoid corniness.

### H. Pricing
Free + Pro subscription. Subscription-first framed as *trust* move.

### I. Founder craft
**Tied with Cursor for highest.** Custom typeface, "invisible brand" philosophy, citation-at-top UX, subscription over ads, boundless-book logo, lowercase "p," refusal of "chatbot." Every choice a *statement*.

## 6. v0 by Vercel — `v0.app`

### A. Positioning & promise
- **Hero:** "What do you want to create?"
- **Subhead:** "Prompt. Build. Publish."
- **CTA:** "New Chat"
- **Category:** "Build Agents, Apps, and Websites with AI"
- **Named user:** Developers, designers, entrepreneurs.

### B. Visual language
- Color: light/neutral with rotating integration-icon strip; v0 inherits Vercel's restrained palette.
- Type: clean modern sans, near-system; Vercel's Geist-family.
- Density: moderate. Whitespace-heavy hero.
- **AI tropes: strikingly absent.** No sparkles. No gradient text. No glow.
- Dark mode: both supported; light feels default.

### C. Making intelligence tangible
**The prompt box IS the hero.** "What do you want to create?" is both copy and CTA. Intelligence demonstrated by *result* — templates gallery shows what v0 actually builds.

### D. Information hierarchy
1. Prompt input → 2. "Prompt. Build. Publish." → 3. Template gallery of real outputs.

### E. Trust mechanics
Inherits Vercel's trust. *"Connect to GitHub and push code directly to your repository,"* *"v0 plans, creates tasks, and connects to databases as it builds."* Trust = *infrastructure verbs*.

### F. Magic moment
Prompt box + template gallery.

### G. Voice
- "What do you want to create?"
- "Prompt. Build. Publish."
- "Generate working applications in minutes with AI. Publish as live websites in seconds."

**Earned:** "Prompt. Build. Publish." — cleanest three-word product narrative.

### H. Pricing
Public, granular. Free ($0, $5 credits, 7 msg/day) → Team ($30/user/mo) → Business ($100/user/mo) → Enterprise. **Per-token model pricing on public site:** v0 Mini at $1/$5 per 1M tokens to v0 Max Fast at $30/$150. **Most transparent AI-cost framing in the set.**

### I. Founder craft
High, inherited from Vercel.

## Synthesis

### Dominant visual archetype
Two coexisting clusters. **Cursor / v0 / Perplexity / Notion-AI:** dark-mode-default (Perplexity neutral), oversized clean sans, restrained gradients as *backdrops*, near-monochrome with one accent. **Glean / Hex:** light-mode enterprise B2B, blue dominant, conventional SaaS pacing. Lab-feel signals "frontier"; enterprise signals "buyable."

### AI-cringe patterns to flag
1. **Sparkle/wand iconography.** Hex's 🪄. *Canva-AI* not *frontier-AI*.
2. **Gradient text on the headline.** Glean and Notion AI. Dates fast.
3. **"Supercharge / magic / unleash" verbs.** Hex's "Bringing the magic of AI."
4. **Anthropomorphizing AI as people.** Notion's "24/7 AI team."
5. **AI-face GIFs.** Notion AI cheapens the surface.
6. **Glow effects on every UI element.**

### Patterns that make intelligence feel earned
1. **Show real output, not iconography.** Cursor puts IDE on homepage. Hex puts real Q&A. v0 puts prompt box.
2. **Citations / traceability.** Perplexity moved citations *to top*. Hex makes every conversation re-openable as notebook.
3. **Type that doesn't ask for attention.** Perplexity's FK Display, Cursor's restrained sans.
4. **Restraint about pricing.** v0 and Cursor publish per-token economics — signals operator-confidence.
5. **Refusing the "chatbot" frame.** Perplexity's "answer engine."

### SMART vs FAST vs MORE
- **FAST:** v0 ("in seconds"), Notion ("while you sleep"). Risk: ages into noise.
- **MORE:** Notion ("infinite minds"), Glean ("110 hours saved"). Risk: 2023 productivity pitch.
- **SMART:** Cursor ("predict your next action"), Perplexity ("trust the answers"), Hex (transparency). Sell *judgment*. **This is what Lead Radar wants.**

### "Thinking partner" vs "tool"
Single clearest signal: **does the product surface ITS REASONING in a way a human can inspect?** For Lead Radar: when the system says *"contact X at company Y is the right warm path,"* it must show the chain — the LinkedIn signal, the job-change date, the originating event. That trace is the trust mechanic.

### What Lead Radar can borrow without inheriting AI-bro tropes
1. **From Perplexity:** *invisible brand* discipline. No sparkles. No gradient hero text. Treat citations as first-class UI.
2. **From Cursor:** dark-mode-default with chromatic backdrop. Real product surface in marketing hero. Honest pricing including per-request economics.
3. **From Hex:** *traceability* move — every AI suggestion re-openable as underlying signal-graph. Bypasses the hallucination conversation.
4. **From v0:** *prompt-is-the-hero* pattern.
5. **From Glean:** one concrete time-saved stat ("Lead Radar surfaces N qualified leads/week with full provenance").
6. **Avoid from Notion AI:** anthropomorphic "24/7 AI team." Lead Radar's voice should be analyst-grade Dutch newsroom, not Silicon Valley copilot.

Category-level lesson: in May 2026, every AI product has a chat box. The ones that feel SMART show their work, refuse the sparkle, publish honest economics. Lead Radar's edge is provenance — make that the visible aesthetic.
